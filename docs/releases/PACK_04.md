# World 04 — The Mostly Clockwork Circus

**Twenty circus rescues. Eighty playable scenes across four worlds.**

Tick wants the show precisely on time. Bea would like a moment to breathe.
Tock discovers that a good drumroll can include a very satisfying pause.

- Twenty authored puzzles: a giraffe-sized entrance, a splash with extravagant
  towel service, a breathtakingly ordinary table, and a biscuit-centered orbit.
- Tock's rhythm desk accepts actual water during generous visible GO windows.
  REST windows never erase progress or count as mistakes. Pause stops the beat.
- Saved untimed assist holds beat gates open, while temperature and pressure
  still matter. Reduced-motion visitors start untimed unless they choose otherwise.
- Both motion and rhythm assists are reachable from Pause with a controller,
  as well as from their scene buttons. An independent review caught this gap.
- A slow oval rehearsal wheel combines moving brass care with gentle stationary
  ribbons. Saved stationary assist remains available.
- Original clock-headed ringmaster, brass giraffe, metronome beetle, circus
  props, striped big top, and completion stamps bring the ensemble together.
- Short silly dispatches and freely selectable scenes: no lives, no deadline,
  no locked progression, and no account.

Adapted from the reviewed World 04 cards in [02-06.json](../campaign/02-06.json),
using the shared [authoring contract](../LEVEL_AUTHORING.md). Decorative
performances happen in completion reveals; the table is not a balance simulation.
