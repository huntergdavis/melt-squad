# World 18 — Apocalypse, Rescheduled

Twenty small repairs for a surprisingly manageable apocalypse. Ann, an anxious
paperwork moth, meets four ponies of mild inconvenience and a pocket-sized giant.
The ominous notice turns out to announce an end-of-day picnic. Bring snacks.

- Twenty source-authored rescues, original local illustrations, earned postcards.
- Redirect actual soup through three channels: build two ice plugs, fill the
  raised middle overflow, then remelt the plugs to reopen the side tributaries.
  In-flight water checks the current ice state before delivery.
- Grow and trim three ice-prism heights continuously. One real refracted beam
  must reach all three star detectors. Notches guide the player; they do not
  trigger a hidden success flag. Winning preserves the actual partial ice.
- Three sequential tea-chime bells with visible pulse rings and saved untimed play.
- A marshmallow meteor, eight-armed tea service, seven walrus invitations, and
  a giant trusted with a very small job. Ann finally switches her clipboard off.

The optical extension reuses bounded convex-polygon Snell refraction, not a
second ray animation. Ice grows within marked moulds; this is not unrestricted
fluid or free-form ice sculpting. Other scenes are authored domestic vignettes.
