# World 05 — Borough of Very Small Affairs

**Twenty tiny civic adventures. One hundred scenes across five worlds.**

Mayor Midge wants every form filed. Clerk Quill has excellent handwriting.
Inspector Pin discovers the most important regulation: leave room for a friend.

- Twenty authored scenes, 83 targets, original mouse/hedgehog/beetle cast,
  oversized stationery, silly approval stamps, and a warm town-hall finale.
- Fill water and build ice to balance a real equal-arm scale. Actual accumulated
  mass drives torque, damping, and settling; the certificate waits for a level
  beam, not just a pair of checked boxes. Loads cap at a forgiving equal mass.
- Build two ice mirrors, then melt an opening for sunshine. Geometric ray
  intersections and reflections light the detector; unbuilt mirrors and actual
  remaining ice cells block success. No authored fake beam-completion flag.
- Visible pans, load bars, settling cues, mirror faces, and a glowing light
  path show the mechanics. Pause freezes their simulation too.
- Quietly funny dispatches: biscuit certification, puddle residency, and an
  office that learns to offer a chair before asking for a signature.

Adapted from the reviewed World 05 cards in [02-06.json](../campaign/02-06.json)
and the shared [authoring contract](../LEVEL_AUTHORING.md). The mass-balance and
reflection slices do not claim future buoyancy, refraction, or phase support.
All new cast and props are original code-native art; existing open-asset
acknowledgements remain available in Credits.
