# World 03 — Brine & Parcel

**Twenty underwater postal puzzles. Sixty playable scenes across three worlds.**

Clack keeps the post office running. Nori worries about express delivery. Dot
sends invitations to herself—until the whole reef discovers her picnic.

- Bubble postage, porcelain compliments, registered soup, and seven very
  particular sorting slots: twenty individually authored layouts and payoffs.
- A real Y-junction water route: fill the far basin, thaw the near gate, then
  share the flow. Direct spraying cannot bypass the pipe puzzle.
- Follow the express snail's gentle care pad, or enable saved stationary
  assist. No deadline and no punishment for taking your time.
- Original crab, eel, octopus, snail, mailbox, shell, diving bell, parcel,
  anemone, and bubble artwork. A reef sorting-office backdrop, character reveals, and
  twenty silly completion stamps connect the calls into one friendly story.
- The atlas gains a third playable world without changing original save IDs.
- Short characterful dispatches replace design-brief prose. Phone mission
  controls sit below the story, leaving the title and introduction full width.

Source: the reviewed World 03 cards in [02-06.json](../campaign/02-06.json),
following the shared [level-authoring contract](../LEVEL_AUTHORING.md).
This underwater setting uses the game's normal arcade water physics.
An independent design review checked all twenty source cards and 74 targets;
its visual corrections made the brass bell recognizable and gave the picnic
all eight promised place settings.
