# World 02 — The Midnight Sock Exchange

**A new world of twenty rescues. Forty playable scenes in total.**

Pearl's ghost laundromat opens at midnight. Lefty the detective sock and Pip
the lint moth discover that a good partner doesn't have to be an identical one.

- Twenty individually laid-out puzzles, from a very important receipt to a
  whole community's exchange night. Warm silk gently; give the oven mitt a
  bolder press; build a coat's completely unnecessary hat stand.
- Rinse River: open channels and feed the inlet. Water visibly travels to its
  destination; spraying directly into the tubs cannot bypass the route.
- Cuff Carousel: follow two real moving care pads with different temperature
  and pressure needs. Optional stationary assist is saved in your browser.
- Original Pearl, Lefty, Pip, garment, basket, and washer artwork; completion
  postcards, rescue stamps, and character reveals.
- Rescue atlas: twenty destinations, winding twenty-scene world maps, quick
  Continue, a compact scene list, keyboard/gamepad/touch controls, and preserved
  original medals. Unbuilt worlds are clearly marked Coming later.
- Credits screen with locally hosted Kenney and font license acknowledgements.

## Release gate

All forty scenes are solved through actual emitted water by the gameplay
tests. Additional checks cover routing, motion/assist, old saves, map progress,
browser navigation, mobile overflow, credits, and simulated standard gamepads.
Physical-controller feel still needs player feedback.

Deploy to `main`, wait for the matching GitHub Pages run, verify the live
`release.json` marker and world/scene UI, then publish this release. The release
body records the verified commit and pipeline URL.
