# World 08 — Emberborough Town Hall

**Twenty dragon-sized welcomes. One hundred sixty scenes across eight worlds.**

Mayor Cinder would like everyone to feel welcome. Flint would like to find
his desk under the forms. The municipal cat has no paperwork whatsoever.

- Twenty original civic rescues, from a puddle permit to a ribbon held by
  one very large mayor and one very small clerk standing on a cat dragon.
- A varied dragon community with different sizes and comfortable temperatures.
  Opposite-weather neighbors can both get what they need; the game asks for
  thoughtful hose settings, not one temperature for everybody.
- Huge queue tickets, a moustache fountain, a whispering complaint horn,
  a speech of excellent length, and tea in thimble-to-bucket portions.
- Actual drainage: freeze the paperwork branch's plug, fill the source basin,
  and keep pouring. Only connected overflow reaches the garden reservoir.
- Actual sunshine: thaw a shutter, freeze two triangular prisms, and refract
  the beam through their physical boundaries onto the bench detector.
  The detector—not a ceremonial extra button or warming pad—finishes the rescue.
- Original brass-and-stone civic art, funny completion stamps, all twenty
  calls freely selectable, saved progress, and in-game local-asset credits.

Adapted from World 08 in [07-11.json](../campaign/07-11.json). Council speeches,
residents, the fountain and opening ceremony are authored ending presentations;
the declared drainage and sunlight exhibits simulate their actual mechanics.
There is no microphone input, general fluid simulation, or spectral dispersion.
