# World 16 — Last Exit Before Breakfast

Twenty stops at a roadside diner near the edge of the universe. Miso has eight
arms and could still use a break. Zip's comet truck can wait. Mallow is a pancake
who would like to become a moon—and discovers a smaller, useful way to shine.

- Twenty source-authored puzzles, original local illustrations, and earned postcards.
- A continuous three-pancake syrup route: open the two icy outlets, fill the
  reservoir, and let actual routed surplus travel through each full upstream basin.
  Downstream basins cannot be completed by spraying them directly.
- Three visible breakfast rhythm gates. Off-beat progress stays put; saved
  untimed mode holds the next light open until its turbine is finished.
- Polar and sun-courier comfort settings, a thank-you pebble's excellent seat,
  a butter-pat salute, and an order that simply asks for somewhere to sit.
- The last rescue saves Miso a place at the shared table. No hurried departure,
  lost lives, waiting-customer timer, or requirement to finish the optional annexes.

ordered untimed assist. Cooking, celestial postures, serving, and parked vehicles
are authored vignettes—not new chemistry, gravity, or spaceflight simulations.
