# World 17 — Toybox After Bedtime

Twenty tiny emergencies after the bedroom lights go out. Captain Button is
a one-eyed plush rabbit. Jun is a tin duck with extremely serious paperwork.
Glim is a wind-up star learning that small is a very good size to be.

- Twenty source-authored rescues, original local toy illustrations, and earned postcards.
- Grow and trim actual ice ballast repeatedly. Button and a feather admiral ride
  the hinged seesaw; the ice cups move with it. Real mass, torque, and settling
  decide victory—not filling both cups or completing a hidden checklist.
- Melt three biscuit moons on genuinely separate slow orbits, with visible
  mechanical arms and saved stationary assist. The Duck Nebula was always there,
  according to Jun.
- One wooden block of emergency response, an exceptionally long dog, diplomatic
  dollhouse tea, a visibly string-powered rocket, and a sock ghost's spare mitten.
- Glim leads the final nightlight parade. Button learns the pleasure of following.
