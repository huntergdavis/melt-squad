# World 07 — Fossilbean Preschool

**Twenty little-dinosaur adventures. One hundred forty scenes across seven worlds.**

Principal Fern has reading glasses and a very important nap agenda. Pebble
would like his tail mitten. Dapple is making room for everybody's family photo.

- Twenty authored nursery, garden, kitchen, cave, and planetarium rescues.
  Original triceratops, ankylosaur, diplodocus, pterosaur, and classroom art.
- A real unequal-arm seesaw: three units at one arm balance one unit at three
  arms. Ice adds actual mass. Completion waits for opposing torque and a resting
  level beam, not simply a finished construction checkbox. Pause holds it still.
- Thaw a start plaque to begin the slow blanket mobile. Actual targets and
  connecting arms move together; completed work persists between revolutions.
  The saved stationary assist remains available in Pause.
- Fern-shaped fern leaves, pretend tooth flowers, story-attending bats, the
  moon's paper crown, and formal socks worn proudly over triceratops horns.
- Dapple curls into a living portrait border for the family open day.
- Freely selectable scenes, original completion stamps, local credits, and
  all existing keyboard, touch, and standard-controller controls.

Adapted from World 07 in [07-11.json](../campaign/07-11.json), using the
[authoring contract](../LEVEL_AUTHORING.md). Only the seesaw and declared
mobile simulate movement; classroom performances are completion presentations.
