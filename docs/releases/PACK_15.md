# World 15 — The Institute of Almost Possible

Twenty exhibits. One axolotl curator. One folded-paper assistant. One excellent
nothing who deserves a curator's ribbon. Dr. Probably, Margin, and Zero reopen
a museum that values good questions more than perfect certainty.

- Twenty source-authored puzzles and earned rescue postcards.
- Two real ice apertures admit a fixed light beam to a question-mark detector.
  A visible beam alone is not the finish: both marked panes must also be cleared.
- A prebuilt floating footnote rises with actual displaced-water buoyancy.
  Fill its one fixed tank; full capacity, force equilibrium, low velocity, and
  sustained rest inside the visible eight-pixel band all matter. No added build chore.
- A steam-question kettle, increasingly square toast, next Tuesday's cake fossil,
  a whale's tiny admission ticket, and a properly credited paper assistant.
- Original local Canvas illustrations, no backend, unchanged local saves, and
  in-game credits for the existing open assets.

The source's legacy BALANCE tag for the floating-footnote annex is implemented
with the already shipped Archimedes buoyancy solver, not a torque balance.
Explicit `prebuilt: true` is mutually exclusive with a construction target.
The sports podium keeps its build-first behavior and half-pixel dock precision;
the new bounded height-band option measures success without adding forces.

Zero does not attract water. Upside-down furniture does not reverse gravity.
Plant growth, moon placement, specimen transformations, and visitors are authored
completion vignettes. Both physical annexes are optional paths, not finale locks.

Release gate: bounded real-water gameplay tests, source-setting review, earned
art inspection, desktop/phone browser checks, production build, exact-commit
CI/Pages success, and public marker/asset/atlas/finale/credits verification.
Publish `pack-15` only after those gates pass.

relay checks. The final four Institute/release checks passed again after the
