# World 06 — The Great Pudding Republic

**Twenty breakfast adventures. One hundred twenty scenes across six worlds.**

President Wobble thinks breakfast can welcome every course. Captain Crumb has
brought a signature crumb. Sprinkle is making room for three different opinions
about napkins, a soup dumpling, and a very crunchy choir.

- Twenty authored pantry puzzles, 82 targets, 83 recipe steps, and original pudding, biscuit, jellybean,
  éclair, dumpling, strawberry, pancake, and utensil art.
- Build an ice mold, fill its basin, then actually melt the same completed
  construction open. Earned phase signals enforce the recipe; a build/fill/open
  strip and changing objective names show what to do next. Ordinary completed
  structures retain their stability.
- Syrup-colored water follows a real connected channel to the far pancake.
  Once it is full, melt the spillway plug and send surplus back to the nearer
  pancake. Direct pouring cannot bypass either routed basin.
- Contrasting custard/cocoa service, layered cake archives, accessible biscuit
  seating, three icing services, an inclusive menu, and a shared breakfast finale.
- All scenes freely selectable, funny completion stamps, local credits,
  keyboard/touch/controller controls, and no punishment for trying again.
- A long-channel fix preserves parcels already in transit under the same
  350-parcel cap; sustained high-pressure inflow can no longer starve a distant
  basin by repeatedly discarding its approaching water.
- Pause now snapshots the throttled HUD immediately. The first candidate's CI
  caught the old clock display refreshing one second after simulation had
  already stopped; the assertion remains strict and unchanged.
